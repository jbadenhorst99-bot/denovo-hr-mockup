The Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA) gives you a right to ask a private body for a record, if you need it to exercise or protect a right. This page is how you ask De Novo Human Resources.Die Wet op die Bevordering van Toegang tot Inligting 2 van 2000 (PAIA) gee jou die reg om ’n rekord te vra as jy dit nodig het om ’n reg uit te oefen of te beskerm.UMthetho Wokukhuthaza Ukufinyelela Olwazini 2 ka-2000 (PAIA) ukunika ilungelo lokucela irekhodi uma ulidinga ukuze usebenzise noma uvikele ilungelo.
