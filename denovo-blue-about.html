De Novo Human Resources is both a human resources firm and a specialised labour law firm. That mix is the point. The firm can see what the business needs to deliver, and it does not trade those deliverables away just to look compliant. The work is to reduce risk and still get the best from employees.De Novo Human Resources is sowel ’n menslikehulpbronfirma as ’n gespesialiseerde arbeidsregfirma. Die mengsel is die punt. Die firma sien wat die besigheid moet lewer, en ruil daardie uitkomste nie net om voldoenend te lyk nie. Die werk is om risiko te verminder en steeds die beste uit werknemers te kry.I-De Novo Human Resources iyinkampani ye-HR neyomthetho wezabasebenzi. Lokho kuyinhloso. Ibona okufanele ibhizinisi likulethe, ingakushi umphumela ukuze kubukeke sengathi kuyahambisana nomthetho. Umsebenzi ukunciphisa ubungozi kodwa kusatholakale okuhle kubasebenzi.

The practice has a footprint in every sector. It recently amalgamated with Naidoo Human Resources, a firm officially in existence since 2013, and operational for more than 20 years. De Novo HR is based in KwaZulu-Natal, with a national footprint and international clients. It is known for building HR departments, outsourced human resources, HR strategy, and labour law interventions.Die praktyk het ’n voetspoor in elke sektor. Dit het onlangs saamgesmelt met Naidoo Human Resources, ’n firma wat amptelik sedert 2013 bestaan en al meer as 20 jaar werksaam is. De Novo HR is in KwaZulu-Natal gesetel, met ’n nasionale voetspoor en internasionale kliënte.Lo msebenzi ukhona kuzo zonke izimboni.
